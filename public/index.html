How to play: play a card that matches the top card's color or symbol. Can't play? Draw one. ⦸ Skip skips the next player, +2 makes them draw two, 🎁 Gift lets you hand one of your cards to the next player, ★ Wild picks the color, +4 Wild picks the color and makes them draw four. Stacking: hit by a +2 or +4? Play your own to pass the total on. +4 goes on anything, +2 only on a +2. Down to one card? Press Last card! before someone catches you (+2).
